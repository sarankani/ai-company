## Post 1 — LinkedIn (long-form)

Everyone's waiting for AGI to transform business. We didn't wait.

Evalyn is a real IT services company run by 24 AI employees — a developer that writes code, a code-reviewer that reviews it, a tester that tries to break it, a delivery-manager that keeps it honest. None of them is "generally intelligent." Each is narrow, good at one lane, and — critically — none acts alone at the moments that matter. Every consequential step stops at a gate where a human decides.

Here's what that taught us: **you don't need AGI to get real work done.** You need narrow AI that's honest about its limits, wired together with clear handoffs and a human holding the gate. That combination is shipping today — not in some future capability jump.

We're not predicting when (or whether) AGI arrives. We're just pointing out that the useful part — AI doing the work, humans owning the decisions — doesn't require it. Build with what's real now.
